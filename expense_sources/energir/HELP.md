Download the two-page bilingual PDF bill from your Énergir customer account and
load it through Expenses > Load expense statement. Review the current-period
natural-gas charge including GST and QST, service dates, identity, category and
optional association before confirming.

The importer uses the gas-period subtotal and taxes, not the previous balance,
an automatic payment, an equal-payment instalment or the total amount due.
The header, current-charge period and detailed period must agree. Both endpoint
dates are included in Énergir's printed day count; no Hydro-style date shift is applied.

The initial supported layout uses year-first dates and Énergir's two-letter month
codes. Bundled bills, rental charges, corrected bills, equal-payment plans, scans,
and unverified layouts require manual entry. Ambiguous dates or inconsistent
amounts fail without creating facts. Older day-first French layouts are not supported.

ForeView does not retain the PDF. It retains its filename, hash, parser version
and the confirmed factual expense. Reimports use the shared overlap-review workflow.
