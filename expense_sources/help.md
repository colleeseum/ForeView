Expense-source modules detect supported statement PDFs and extract the current-period
amount, billing period, and provider metadata without storing the original document.
Preview every value before confirming. Confirmed records enter factual expense history
with full provenance. Montréal annual municipal-tax accounts represent the billed
annual obligation, not installments or payment evidence. Synthetic Hydro-Québec,
Énergir and Montréal municipal-tax generators are available
for automated testing; only documented, verified layouts are supported.
