Download your annual City of Montréal municipal-tax account, then open
Expenses > Load expense statement. The verified layouts are the English and French two-page
PDF with a full calendar-year taxation period and two scheduled installments.
Other layouts require separate verification. Document language is independent of
interface language; both layouts retain the same suggested identity for the same property.

The imported amount is the billed annual obligation, not a payment or the current
outstanding balance. The printed tax-charge lines and the two installments must
each reconcile to the account total. Detachable coupons and the optional
interest-bearing payment plan on the reverse are not additional expenses.
The annual amount is never extrapolated as a recurring utility estimate.

Review the year, property address and account number in the suggested identity.
Confirm your category and real-estate association before saving. No property is
matched automatically, and taxable assessments never update property valuations.
Use the same identity, category and association for manual or installment evidence
of this obligation so shared overlap review can prevent double counting. Repeated
identical PDFs also trigger review even with a renamed identity.

ForeView retains the filename, document hash, parser version and confirmed expense,
not the PDF. Corrected/supplementary bills, partial-year accounts, balance statements,
receipts, transfer duties, school taxes and scanned documents are unsupported.

City guidance distinguishes the annual account from payment coupons and later payments:
[Tax account](https://montreal.ca/sujets/compte-de-taxes) and
[Get a copy](https://montreal.ca/demarches/consulter-une-copie-de-son-compte-de-taxes).
